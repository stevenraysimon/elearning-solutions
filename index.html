Frequently Asked Questions

What types of organizations do you work with?

I work with organizations of all sizes — from small businesses and nonprofits to universities and large corporations. If you have learners and content, I can build something for you.

What LMS platforms do you support?

I deliver SCORM-compliant courses that work with virtually any LMS — Brightspace/D2L, Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, and more. If you're not sure about your platform, we can figure it out together.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the scope. A custom tool or small game might be ready in a week or two. A full course with audio, interactions, and assessments typically takes 3–6 weeks. We'll set a clear timeline when we talk.

Do you provide the content, or do I?

Most clients provide their subject matter content and I handle all the design, development, audio production, and technical build. If you need help structuring your content or writing scripts, I can assist with that too.

What does an eLearning game look like?

It can be anything — a Family Feud-style quiz game, a branching scenario storybook, a drag-and-drop sorting challenge, or even a 3D environment your learners explore. We'll find the format that fits your content and your audience.

How do AI eLearning solutions work?

AI solutions can range from a chatbot that answers learner questions in real time, to adaptive experiences that adjust difficulty based on performance, to AI-powered tools that help your team create content faster. We'll explore what makes sense for your goals.

How do I get started?

Just hit "Get a Quote" and fill out a quick form. I'll reach out to schedule a call and we'll go from there. No commitment required — let's just talk about your project.
